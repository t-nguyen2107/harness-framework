# Decision log

Owned by **pm**. Append-only, newest at the bottom. Log an answer when it changes scope,
behaviour, an acceptance criterion, a data model, or a rule other tasks must follow. Do not log
routine clarifications.

Agents read this before starting work: a decision here overrides an older line in a story,
task or TC file.
Decisions that outlive the story are promoted to ADRs in `memory/decisions/` at closure.
